# Writing Skill

Use this skill when you draft or edit marketing copy, product/UI copy, decks, or reports. It condenses PLAYBOOK.md; see that file for full citations and coverage limits. Tags are [ID Title, p. N]. "Rec:" marks our recommendations, which are not source rules.

## Coverage limits (read first)

- 58 of 64 brand guides have note sections. 068 and 120 are restricted and excluded. 111, 115, 137, and 138 have no notes.
- 059 is excluded (attribution unresolved). 074 is held back (its notes appear duplicated from 028/031).
- Reports: 22 sections from a sample stopped at 22/30. It is partial, not representative.
- Figures in sources are historical. Never reuse them as current facts.
- Korean-specific style rules are unverified except the ones tagged below.

## Step 1: Frame before drafting

- Identify the audience, their knowledge, and their state [081 Zapier Brand Guide 2022, pp. 20, 23].
- Pick the register by channel: formal for proposals, conversational for social [102 hcma Brand Guidelines, p. 12]; [311 University of Michigan Brand Identity Style Guide 2025, p. 12].
- Pick the stage: inspire, engage, or inform [171 Docusign Brand Guidelines, pp. 77-78]; product-use copy stays plain [132 Freepik Brand Guidelines, pp. 27-28].
- Keep the voice fixed and flex the tone [083 Oyster Brand Guidelines, p. 11].
- Write tone traits as "X, but not Y" pairs [061 Lunchbox Brand Guidelines, p. 7]; [084 Ikan Brand Guidelines 2023, p. 10].

## Step 2: Headline

- State the takeaway, not a topic [062 Investor Day 2022 Deck, pp. 9, 79]; [323 Uber Investor Update February 2024, pp. 8-9].
- Lead with the reader's benefit [046 Bolt Brand Guidelines, p. 8]; [314 CTAO Brand Guidelines, p. 23].
- Keep it short, in sentence case, without all caps [032 Monzo Brand Style Guide Deck, pp. 35, 39]; [244 Buffer Brand Guide, p. 1]; [082 NorthAlley Brand Guidelines, p. 24].
- A quantified headline needs metric, direction, period, and driver [069 Palantir Q1 2023 Business Update, pp. 9-14]; [349 KakaoBank 2Q26 IR Presentation, pp. 5-6].
- The headline must agree with the chart or data [124 Dropbox Q3 2024 Conference Call Deck, p. 18].
- Pair a playful headline with a functional subhead [096 Pleo Brand Guidelines v4.0, pp. 21-23].
- End punctuation is house style: full stop [096 Pleo Brand Guidelines] vs none [132 Freepik Brand Guidelines, pp. 67-68]. Rec: pick one per brand.

## Step 3: Body

- Main point first, everyday words, few modifiers, acronyms explained [096 Pleo Brand Guidelines v4.0, p. 16].
- Use active voice and sentences of about 15-20 words [102 hcma Brand Guidelines, pp. 11, 13]; [311 University of Michigan Brand Identity Style Guide 2025, p. 29].
- Keep paragraphs to 2-4 sentences [118 OurFamilyWizard Brand Guidelines, p. 20].
- Support claims with facts, stats, or quotes [061 Lunchbox Brand Guidelines, p. 72]; pair each message with a concrete example [309 Smithsonian Identity Guidelines, p. 14].
- Bold at most two word groups per sentence [036 Widelab Brand Guidelines, p. 30]; use emphasis sparingly [078 Olympic Brand Guidelines, p. 52].
- Use at most one exclamation mark; write "for example", not "eg" [102 hcma Brand Guidelines, pp. 13, 66].
- Humor only if it is clear and relevant, with no mood-only puns [046 Bolt Brand Guidelines 2023, pp. 10-13]; [096 Pleo Brand Guidelines, p. 19].
- Use inclusive language and no stereotypes [031 Pro League Brand Guidelines, p. 46]; [118 Our Family Wizard Brand Book 2024, p. 20].

## Step 4: CTA

- Make it short, literal, and distinct: "Book now" [310 British Museum Design Guidelines (2012), p. 12]; "Start earning" [028 Mash's Brand Guidelines].
- Make it specific, with a clear outcome [096 Pleo Brand Guidelines, p. 17].
- Sign-up and instruction copy stays straightforward [046 Bolt Brand Guidelines 2023, pp. 54, 58]; be firm but friendly, never scolding [192 Chinatown Brand Guidelines, p. 20].
- Decks: offer a self-serve action plus a sales option [029 Linear Pitch Deck, p. 10].
- Rec: use verb plus outcome in 2-4 words, matched to the page promise.

## Step 5: Evidence rules (decks and reports)

- Every figure gets period, unit, basis, definition, and source [324 Uber Investor Day 2022, pp. 9, 31, 56]; [317 DoorDash 2021 Investor Presentation, pp. 4, 9-11].
- Use % for growth and pp for margins [343 Kurly 4Q25 Earnings Presentation, pp. 5, 12].
- Label estimates, forecasts, and illustrations [048 Palantir Q2 2021 Business Update, pp. 2, 15, 41]; [347 Robinhood Investor Day 2024, pp. 36, 80].
- Label measured, estimated, and modeled figures [203 Google Environmental Report 2025, pp. 58-60, 81-82]; contracted vs delivered [202 Microsoft Environmental Sustainability Report 2025, pp. 11, 24, 28].
- Give every percentage a denominator [232 Siemens Sustainability Report 2024, pp. 2, 26, 38-39, 60]; [228 Walmart ESG Report FY2026, pp. 4-5, 30].
- Define boundaries before headline claims [225 Coca-Cola Environmental Update 2024]; keep results apart from targets [233 Maersk Sustainability Report 2023].
- Show adverse totals with positive headlines [218 NVIDIA Sustainability Report FY2026, pp. 3-4, 11-12].
- Report missed targets plainly [220 Workday Global Impact Report 2025, pp. 22-23].
- Separate findings from recommendations and pitches [134 DEPT Trends Report 2025, pp. 2-4, 29, 57].
- Anecdotes are not prevalence; include counter-evidence [151 D&AD Trend Report 2024, pp. 2, 27, 33].
- If no metric exists, describe the outcome in words and never invent one [103 BB Agency Company Deck, pp. 9, 25].
- Replace superlatives with dated, sourced comparators [350 Toss Logistics Company Presentation 2023, pp. 5, 14, 16].

## Step 6: Names

- Use plain descriptors under the parent brand, with no new sub-brands [047 Formula 1 Brand Guidelines, p. 186]; [310 British Museum Design Guidelines 2012, p. 4].
- Use the full name first, then shorten [303 BAW Architecture Brand Guidelines, p. 3].
- Typeset names in text; never insert the logo [132 Freepik Brand Guidelines 2023, pp. 37, 68]; [043 OpenAI Brand Guidelines, p. 11].
- Use approved forms only; no mixed languages or invented abbreviations [348 KakaoBank Brand Guidelines (naming only), p. 3].

## Step 7: Korean

- Sourced: numbered English label plus Korean claim headline in IR decks [331 Samjin Foods IPO IR Book (2025), pp. 19, 21, 28]; [341 Kurly Investor Relations Deck 2025, pp. 7, 19, 21].
- Sourced: a Korean claim with an English parallel on dividers [350 토스 회사소개서 2023, pp. 3-4, 7].
- Sourced: 여 marks an approximate amount; keep exact and approximate counts apart [356 Yanolja Business Introduction (2018), pp. 6, 9-12].
- Unverified (Rec): one consistent sentence ending per piece; active verbs over translated passives; no double passives; check spacing; rewrite rather than translate English headlines. Have a native editor confirm.

## Step 8: Final check

- [ ] The headline is a claim, and its numbers carry a basis.
- [ ] The benefit comes first, and every claim has nearby proof.
- [ ] There are no unsourced superlatives, competitor brand names, or reused historical figures.
- [ ] Evidence types are labelled, and adverse facts are shown.
- [ ] Sentence case is used, emphasis is minimal, and acronyms are expanded.
- [ ] The CTA is short and literal and matches the promise.
- [ ] Names use the approved forms.
- [ ] Unverified Korean rules have been reviewed.

## Quick before-after

- "The food game was changed by us" -> "We changed the food game." [061 Lunchbox Brand Guidelines, p. 8]
- The guide rejects mood-only wordplay in favor of a specific customer benefit [046 Bolt Brand Guidelines 2023, pp. 10, 15]
- Generic claim -> a specific researcher example [311 University of Michigan Brand Identity Style Guide 2025, p. 38]
- Rec: "Our innovative platform empowers teams" -> "Send invoices in two clicks and see who has paid."
